The school

Chukwuemeka Odumegwu Ojukwu University is a state university in Anambra State, Nigeria, named after the late Dim Chukwuemeka Odumegwu Ojukwu. Its College of Medicine is in Awka.

History

The university began as Anambra State University, established by the Anambra State Government in 2000 under Law No. 13 of 2000. It was later renamed in honour of Dim Chukwuemeka Odumegwu Ojukwu, and the university law enacted in 2014 is the one that governs it today.

Campuses

COOU has three campuses: Uli, Igbariam and Awka. The Awka campus, at Amaku, is home to the College of Medicine and the Teaching Hospital.

Medicine at COOU

Medical students study for the MBBS degree over six levels, from 100 level to 600 level. Clinical training takes place at the Chukwuemeka Odumegwu Ojukwu University Teaching Hospital (COOUTH) at Amaku, Awka.

The association

COOUMSA is the Medical Students Association of the university. It brings together medical students from 100 level to 600 level and works to make learning materials, timetables and notices easy to find.

What you will find here

Study materials arranged by level and course, timetables and course allocation, an e-library of textbooks, and notices from the association.
